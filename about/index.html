About MIGA Worldwide

A B2B marketplace for international real estate investment.

What we do

MIGA Worldwide is a B2B marketplace for international real estate investment. We connect investors and family offices with developers and real estate opportunities in Europe and Latin America, and we support the first stage of the process with a presentation and review of the commercial information provided by developers.

What we are not

MIGA Worldwide does not provide investment, legal or tax advice and is not a registered broker-dealer or investment adviser. Investors and developers make their own decisions and should rely on their own independent advisers.

Who operates MIGA Worldwide

MIGA Worldwide is a brand operated by NEXILON LLC, a United States limited liability company.
