What we do

A structured first step between investors and developers

We help qualified counterparties find each other across borders and give them the information they need to start a conversation.

Project information overview

We present the information developers provide in a clear format, without recommending any project.

Commercial information review

We review the commercial information that developers share before an introduction is made. This is not an assessment or recommendation of any investment.

Introductions

We connect investors and developers whose objectives fit, so they can talk directly and decide independently.

How it works

Three steps, from first contact to introduction

  1. 01

    Tell us what you are looking for

    Investors share their criteria; developers share their project.

  2. 02

    We review and introduce

    We review the information and introduce parties with compatible interests.

  3. 03

    We make the introduction

    Both parties are introduced and continue the process with their own advisers.

How we work

Our principles

Clear information

Project information is presented as provided by developers, in a consistent and readable format.

Direct relationships

Once introduced, investors and developers deal with each other directly.

Independent decisions

MIGA Worldwide does not provide investment, legal or tax advice and is not a registered broker-dealer or investment adviser. Each party decides with its own advisers.
